## Why Should I Join?

Throughout the year, we host technical workshops, networking events, career
development opportunities, and social events that complement what students learn
in the classroom. Some of our signature events include the PCB Business Card
Workshop, the Fall Resume Review with Marvell and Spring Resume Review with
Samsung SmartThings before each CSE Career Fair, and the Keller LAN Party in
collaboration with the Association of Computing Machinery (ACM UMN) and Gopher
Graphics.

Beyond hosting events, IEEE UMN serves as a voice for the ECE student body. Our
board works closely with faculty and department leadership to represent
student perspectives, communicate concerns, and advocate for initiatives that
improve the undergraduate experience. We strive to build an inclusive community
where students feel supported academically, professionally, and socially.

We also hold weekly general meetings where [members](/membership) can hear club
updates, learn about upcoming opportunities, and help shape the direction of the
organization. General meetings are followed by committee meetings, which offer
members a chance to get more involved in planning events, outreach, projects,
and other initiatives.

Whether you're looking to develop technical skills, prepare for internships,
meet new people, or make an impact within the ECE community, we'd love to have
you. Come to an event, stop by a general meeting, study in [our room], and
introduce yourself!

[our room]: https://campusmaps.umn.edu/kenneth-h-keller-hall

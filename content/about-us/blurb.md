IEEE UMN is the student chapter of the Institute of Electrical and
Electronics Engineers (IEEE) at the University of Minnesota. Our mission
is to support electrical and computer engineering students by creating
opportunities for technical growth, professional development, leadership,
and community.

As part of IEEE--the world's largest professional technical organization
dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity--we connect
students with a global network of engineers, researchers, educators, and
industry professionals.

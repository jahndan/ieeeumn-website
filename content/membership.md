# IEEE UMN Student Membership

Join a community of students looking to grow inside and outside of the classroom
through professional development opportunities and social events!

## Perks

[Keller 2-110]: https://campusmaps.umn.edu/kenneth-h-keller-hall

Our IEEE lounge ([Keller 2-110]) is open 24/7 to eligible student members and includes:

- A kitchenette with a microwave, coffee maker, fridge, and freezer
- Books commonly used in ECE courses
- A community study space, with a TV screen for public use
- Lab equipment for hands-on learning, including oscilloscopes, function
  generators, and bench power supplies

Other benefits can include:

- Voting on certain club-wide decisions, and in the annual officer election
  <!-- besides the elections, sometimes we have polls on preferences -->
- Eligibility to run as a candidate in the annual officer election
- Invitation to the annual IEEE Banquet <!-- what is the banquet? -->
- Club-themed goodies (T-shirts, stickers, etc.)
- Access to Keller Hall 2<sup>nd</sup> floor locker storage

## Renewal

Complete the membership requirements during the school year to gain membership
until October 1<sup>st</sup> of the following school year. As an example, if
your membership begins Sep. 20<sup>th</sup> of 2025, it would last until Oct.
1<sup>st</sup> of 2026.

- Requirements must be completed between the first day of class in the fall term
  and the last day of class in the spring term.
- Partially satisfied requirements do not roll over to the summer term or after,
  so each membership requirement would have to be satisfied between Labor Day
  and Oct. 1<sup>st</sup> to maintain membership benefits continuously.

> **Disclaimer**: Our club membership is not equivalent to the IEEE
> membership (with the parent organization). IEEE UMN student membership
> is exclusive to UMN students and is free, made possible by our sponsors
> and the ECE department. In short, **a separate IEEE membership is
> not required to join IEEE UMN.** If you are interested in acquiring
> membership to the parent organization, you may find [more details on their
> website](https://www.ieee.org/membership).

## Requirements

**Lower Division** (1<sup>st</sup> and 2<sup>nd</sup> year students):

- Attend 1 general meeting (Tuesdays 5:30 to 6pm, Lind L125)
- Attend 1 committee meeting (right after general meetings)
- Attend 1 [IEEE event](/#upcoming-events)

**Upper Division** (3<sup>rd</sup> year students and up):

- Fulfill all lower division requirements
- Attend 1 [ROE](/about-us#outreach-roe-committee) event as a mentor

After completing the requirements, fill out the [membership application
form](https://z.umn.edu/MembershipIEEE) and you will receive membership once
confirmed.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I join?

To participate in our events, just RSVP and show up!

These are the general requirements to become a member:

- Attend one (1) general meeting (Tuesdays at 5:30-5:45pm @ Lind L125)
- Attend one (1) committee meeting (Tuesdays at 5:45-6:30pm, broken out after
  general meetings)
- Attend one (1) event hosted by IEEE (example: resume review, ice cream night,
  etc).

If you are within the major (third year, fourth year, and graduate ECE
students), these are **additional requirements**:

- Attend one (1) ROE workshop event
- Exceptional committee participation (more details given throughout the year)

Then, fill out this [form](https://z.umn.edu/MembershipIEEE) to become a member,
which makes you eligible to vote or run in our annual elections, as well as
granting you card access to our room (Keller 2-110).

## What if I have a course conflict with the general and committee meeting?

If your MyU schedule shows a course timing conflict with the general/committee
meeting time, you may substitute two events' attendance for the general and
committee meetings.

Then, instead of filling out the above form, you would email `ieee@umn.edu`
with your name, x500, and all relevant event attendance. (Note that this still
means 3 total attendances for all students, plus a ROE workshop attendance for
upper division students.)

Please also include a screenshot of your MyU schedule showing the course
conflict schedule.

## When does my membership expire?

All memberships expire October 13. (This includes door access.) The time between
the first day of school and this expiration date serves as a grace period to
renew membership (without losing door access) by attending the required events.

## Where can I see upcoming events?

Any of the following options:

- see the `#announcements` channel in our [discord]
- subscribe to the [newsletter]
- the flyers posted on our room door (Keller 2-110) or the bulletin board in
  front of it
- the calendar on our [website's homepage](https://ieee.umn.edu)

The most up-to-date information will be in our [discord] `#announcements`
channel.

## What are the perks of membership/room access?

[Keller 2-110]: https://campusmaps.umn.edu/kenneth-h-keller-hall

Our IEEE lounge ([Keller 2-110]) is open 24/7 to eligible student members and includes:

- A kitchenette with a microwave, coffee maker, fridge, and freezer
- Books commonly used in ECE courses
- A community study space, with a TV screen for public use
- Lab equipment for hands-on learning, including oscilloscopes, function
  generators, and bench power supplies

Other benefits can include:

- Voting on certain club-wide decisions, and in the annual officer election
  <!-- besides the elections, sometimes we have polls on preferences -->
- Eligibility to run as a candidate in the annual officer election
- Invitation to the annual IEEE Banquet <!-- what is the banquet? -->
- Club-themed goodies (T-shirts, stickers, etc.)
- Access to Keller Hall 2<sup>nd</sup> floor locker storage
